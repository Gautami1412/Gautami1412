## Featured Projects

**[Integrative GWAS × ChIP-seq: Shared Genetic Architecture of CAD & Rheumatoid Arthritis](https://github.com/Gautami1412/GWAS-ChIPseq-CAD-RA-MultiOmics)**
A multi-omics pipeline integrating GWAS (N > 1M), ENCODE H3K27ac ChIP-seq, and eQTL data. Narrowed 53,000+ disease variants to 15 shared regulatory SNPs active in both CD4+ T cells and CD14+ monocytes, and 3 validated HLA-C cis-eQTLs, mechanistic evidence for immune-mediated CAD–RA comorbidity. *Bash · bedtools · GWAS · ChIP-seq · eQTL*

**[mtDNA Heteroplasmy & Preterm Birth Risk](https://github.com/Gautami1412/mtDNA-Heteroplasmy-Analysis-Preterm-Birth-and-Gestational-Age)**
Scalable pipeline across 8,599 maternal samples and 126 mtDNA variants, applying mixed-effects models (site as random effect), LASSO, and Random Forest with SHAP interpretability to dissect population-specific preterm-birth risk. *Python · scikit-learn · statsmodels · mixed-effects modeling*

**[Multi-Ancestry HLA Variant Calling Pipeline](https://github.com/Gautami1412/hla-multi-ancestry-pipeline)** *(in progress)*
A GATK Best Practices pipeline benchmarking reference-genome bias in the HLA region across 1000 Genomes ancestries, scored against Genome in a Bottle truth sets. *Snakemake · BWA-MEM · GATK4 · hap.py · Docker · AWS Batch*

**[Computational Drug Discovery: Natural TPO Inhibitors for Hyperthyroidism](https://github.com/Gautami1412/TPO-Virtual-Screening-Computational-Drug-Discovery)**
End-to-end structure-based virtual screening — homology modeling, molecular docking, and 10 ns MD simulations, identifying Rutin as a low-toxicity lead (−9.6 vs −3.6 kcal/mol; LD50 5000 vs 220 mg/kg) over an FDA-approved drug. *AutoDock Vina · Desmond · SwissADME · PyMOL*

**[Childhood Asthma Risk Modeling](https://github.com/Gautami1412/childhood-asthma-risk-modeling)**
Statistical and ML modeling of childhood asthma on nationally representative NSCH survey data (~21,600 children); compared logistic, LASSO, and random-forest models with cross-validation and calibration (**best AUC ≈ 0.84**). *R · glmnet · caret · randomForest*

➡️ More projects, including leukemia classification from gene expression, RNA-seq analysis of complement genes, and ICU mortality modeling in MIMIC-III, are on my **[portfolio website](https://gautami1412.github.io)**.
